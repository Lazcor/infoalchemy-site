The InfoAlchemy Method

Five stages. One transmutation: expertise into asset.

Nothing gets rebuilt from scratch. What already exists gets found first, then refined into something that generates real, measurable value.

01Gather
Everything that already exists, knowledge, data, expertise, materials, gets collected first. What's missing gets found, then we scale.
02Filter & Organize
Raw information becomes structured. What doesn't matter gets cut. What does get organized into a clear, logical framework, ready to use.
03Communicate
Structure only creates value when the right people understand it. The work gets translated into the language, visuals, and formats the audience actually needs.
04Create
The finished asset gets built: a program, an offer, a framework, a deliverable. Made to be used, not just presented.
05Capitalize
The asset gets positioned to generate real, measurable value: priced, packaged, and deployed to turn the work into results.
How I Do It

The Strategic Leadership Method.

Ten steps, from walking into ambiguity to carrying the plan through to completion.

01
Information Gathering
Walk into an ambiguous situation and gather what's actually there: the people, the politics, the unwritten rules. Turn it into actionable intelligence before you act.
02
Finding Strategic Alignment
Understand the organization's real vision and long-term purpose first. Strategy only matters if it actually supports that purpose.
03
Engage Stakeholders & Define the Parameters
Engage every stakeholder who's actually in the game: the C-suite, executives, team leads, and third-party partners. Understand the real parameters and constraints before you move.
04
The Power of Data-Driven Clarity
Gather the data that already exists, generate what's missing, and test before committing. Operate off what the evidence shows, not assumption.
05
Lead Through Emotional Intelligence
Lead with a genuine read on what the audience, customers, operations teams, and other leaders are telling you about the playing field. Build the communication channels that keep people aligned.
06
Cross-Functional Leadership
Move people and ideas across project, product, and portfolio lines. Lead cross-functional teams and lead upward without a title.
07
Present With Impact
Communicate objectives, data, and strategy to stakeholders and executive leaders, in writing, in speech, and visually. Speak so the room moves.
08
Aligning People & Technology
Human-centered by design: use tools and technology to serve the people and the mission, not the other way around. Every tool applied is built for purpose.
09
Prove It With OKRs & KPIs
Build OKRs and KPIs that track what actually changes the value the business produces, not metrics for the sake of having metrics.
10
Execution Management
Carry the plan through to completion using micro-tests to validate as you go. Build feedback loops so the plan adjusts in real time instead of waiting until the end.
